Coral Bay is a complete open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser. Mara Voss, a getaway driver, is back in her sun-soaked coastal hometown to work off her brother's debt — one stolen car, one police chase, one job at a time. Drive the avenues and the beach road, steal cars, lose the cops, fight on the pier, and run six story jobs from First Wheels to the Clean Getaway. Traffic, pedestrians, a five-star wanted system, police cruisers, on-foot combat, a HUD with a live minimap, a save, and a synthesised radio, engine and sirens — all from static files, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrows to move and drive, E to get in and out of a car, click to shoot on foot, Shift to sprint, M for the job list, R to restart a job, P or Esc to pause. Your progress saves in the browser.
